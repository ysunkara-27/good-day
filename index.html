Google Calendar

Primary calendar · Read only

When connected, task pup reads event titles, times, and availability from your primary calendar to show your schedule and place tasks around busy events. Our backend processes this data and stores encrypted connection credentials. How your data is handled.

Busy timed events reserve space for automatic tasks. All-day and free events stay visible without blocking time. Fixed task times stay put and flag overlaps. Tasks remain in task pup.

Apple Calendar: Google calendars shown in Apple's app work through the Google connection. Direct iCloud calendar connection is not supported yet.
